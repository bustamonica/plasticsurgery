## Training track sharp edges

- Authoritative docs: `training/README.md` (pipeline, RunPod steps) and `training/dataset_schema.json` (consent contract per pair).
- Never commit photos or datasets: `training/data/` is gitignored on purpose; the consented clinic corpus lives outside the repo at `~/firstmate/data/clinic-corpus/` (scraper intake in `<clinic>/raw/`, with `.scraper-cache/` beside it).
- **A pair only counts once it is in the FINISHED tree**, `clinic-corpus/<clinic>/<pair-id>/`. `<clinic>/staging/` and `<clinic>/raw/` are unfinished and invisible to every corpus walk, dataset build and audit - drdanielbarrett, drkolker and sanantonio held 745 pairs in staging and nothing finished until 2026-08-15, and corpus totals quoted anywhere older than that blend the two trees. `training/scripts/emit_corpus.py` is the carry-through (staging -> finished) and the only thing that should write that tree: it re-encodes nothing (`ingest.py` already stripped EXIF, and it refuses rather than strips if an image still carries any), never overwrites an existing pair, and `--report` enumerates every staged pair with its disposition. Always `--dry-run` first. Presence in the finished tree is what makes a pair count, and a documented exclusion does not unmake that: several clinics are ruled out in prose while their pairs sit live in that tree (`training/clinic_watermarks.md`, "Excluded on paper vs excluded in fact").
- Withheld pairs are **retired, not deleted**: `training/retired_pairs.json` enumerates every id kept out of the finished corpus with its ruling and reason (currently the captain's 176 retired laterality labels of 2026-08-15 - 134 sanantonio, 42 drdanielbarrett - plus `sanantonio-24007-oblique-right` withheld as contested, plus the 119 heavenly pairs retired under `retired_watermark` on the captain's 2026-08-15 on-body-watermark ruling, plus 62 pairs over 23 cases and 8 clinics retired under `retired_combined_procedure` on the captain's 2026-08-19 ruling that every combined-procedure case comes out). It is an enumeration and not a rule on purpose: a rule is a live query over staging, and a re-annotation would silently widen a ruling made over a fixed set. `emit_corpus.py` copies each withheld pair into `~/firstmate/data/ba-viz-emit-backlog/quarantine/<reason>/<clinic>/<pair-id>/` and leaves `staging/` untouched; that tree's `MANIFEST.md` is the per-pair record. The registry is the sole authority on a retirement and is keyed by pair id, not by clinic, so `--clinic` cannot be misspelled into letting a retired pair through, and deleting an id from the registry is sufficient to re-emit that pair. The registry gates the EMIT PATH only: an entry keeps a pair from being carried out of `staging/` and does nothing about one already in the finished tree, so removing an already-emitted pair takes a change to the corpus tree itself - do not assume a registry edit is enough. `colville-432-front`/`-side-left` are the worked example: the corrected purity screen caught them a sweep after the original retirement pass, and registering them changed nothing until the pair folders were moved out of the finished tree on 2026-08-19. `--clinic` selects which staged pairs are carried as well as which corpus subdirectory they land in: `ingest.py` stages every clinic into one flat tree, so a staged pair belonging to another clinic is reported as `other-clinic` and left for that clinic's own run rather than misfiled under this one.
- `ClinicConfig.bottom_crop_px` + `crop_bottom()` is how a band burned into the bottom of a side-by-side composite is removed: it trims BOTH halves by the same rows after the split, so the two never differ in framing. Measure the band yourself over every published composite and leave margin - choice's BEFORE/AFTER caption band measures 50-53px and is cropped at 60, leaving 455x441 halves with only ~10% margin over the 400px floor. A band that prints the words BEFORE and AFTER under the respective halves is the label leak in its purest form: the model can satisfy the edit by repainting letters.

- **A watermark on ONE half of a pair and not the other is a label leak, and is CROPPED** (captain, 2026-08-19; `.claude/skills/clinic-prospecting/SKILL.md` Screen 4a is the full lesson). An edit model asked to enlarge the breasts can satisfy that by adding or removing the mark, and every quality gate the pipeline has passes it - it surfaces only as a model that learned nothing. Found on four clinics by averaging every frame per half and high-passing the average (bodies cancel, a fixed-position mark survives): tccs on the BEFORE half (743 pairs), roth, camp and wny on the AFTER half (162/299/10). `ClinicConfig.bottom_crop_px` trims BOTH halves equally as the scraper writes the pair (a collection-time crop, so an affected clinic is re-scraped from cache rather than re-emitted) - tccs 130, roth 175, camp 110, wny 60, each measured per clinic and confirmed constant in pixels rather than as a fraction of height. - The Page 1 Solutions purity screen is two-stage and the second stage needs negation handling. The chart's `Procedure Type` field is taken at face value, but a second procedure often reaches only the narrative (`She underwent removal of her old implants and capsulectomy`), so the narrative is screened too - sentence by sentence, skipping any sentence carrying a negation or hypothetical marker. This surgeon's prose routinely explains what the patient DECLINED (`she did not want to have breast lift ... and opted for a breast augmentation alone`) or what was merely discussed as options; without the negation scope that costs four pure ncps cases outright. Bare `removal` is deliberately not a screen term: it reads as a prior unrelated operation about as often as an explant (`a previous tumor removal in the left breast resulted in volume asymmetry`), and the explant cases it would catch are already caught by `capsulectomy`/`explant`/the revision terms. - **Laterality from a contact sheet is decided by the FAR breast, not by the arm.** CLAUDE.md's convention (chest angled toward the viewer's left => `-left`) is right, but the near-arm phrasing is easy to misapply: on ncps's 480px tiles the arm cue read backwards on two of the first eight cases (10832, 16452) and only a 2x zoom settled them. The cue that does not fail is that **the FAR breast is drawn edge-on against the body's silhouette, on the side the chest faces** - far breast at the frame's right edge => `oblique-right`/`side-right`. Anchored on ncps case 14506, whose tattooed RIGHT forearm is visible in the front view (viewer's left = patient's right) and again as the near arm in its `oblique-right`. The silhouette-centroid statistic confirms a whole clinic's labels as a POPULATION (ncps: left views 0.51-0.52 of frame width, right views 0.47-0.48, front 0.497, matching heavenly's 0.555/0.473) but is useless per case - these frames are individually cropped, so framing dominates pose. - A caption is a training LABEL, so an unrecorded field is omitted, never defaulted. The caption states the three axes the product exposes - exact `volume_cc` (never bucketed or rounded; 10 cc granularity at inference depends on the literal figure), `profile`, and `view` - and drops any clause whose field the clinic did not record. Before 2026-08-16 `profile` defaulted to "a balanced profile" on the 61% of pairs carrying none, which taught the model that an unrecorded outcome was a moderate one, and `view` reached no caption at all, which is why the model could not be asked for a projection. `lib/prompt.ts` must omit on the same rule; a caller that knows the photo's view should pass it, since every training caption carries one.
- `lib/prompt.ts` imports `./implants.ts` with the explicit extension and splits type-only bindings into `import type`. Node's test runner resolves ESM specifiers verbatim and only strips types, so without both, `lib/prompt.test.ts` cannot load the module (`tsconfig.json` sets `allowImportingTsExtensions` for exactly this).
- The website's AI provider seam is `AI_PROVIDER` in `.env.example`; `gemini` is the default and the Gemini prompt in `lib/prompt.ts` (`buildEditPrompt`) is intentionally different from the training-caption format.
- Configurator options the corpus doesn't yet back are gated behind an `unbacked` flag on their `lib/implants.ts` entry (currently extra-high profile, teardrop shape) and hidden by default; flip `NEXT_PUBLIC_ENABLE_UNBACKED_IMPLANT_OPTIONS` (`.env.example`) to show them. Read it via `availableProfiles()`/`availableShapes()`, not `PROFILES`/`SHAPES` directly, in any code that renders or validates selectable options - that keeps the UI (`app/studio/page.tsx`) and the API gate (`app/api/generate/route.ts`) consistent. There is no shared `lib/config.ts`; env vars are read ad hoc at their point of use. Flipping the flag needs no code change, but it does need a rebuild and redeploy to take effect (both for the studio bundle and the `/api/generate` gate): Next.js inlines `NEXT_PUBLIC_*` values into the build output rather than reading them from the running process, unlike `GEMINI_API_KEY`, which is genuinely read at runtime server-side in `app/api/generate/route.ts`.
