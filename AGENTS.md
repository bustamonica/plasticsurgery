## Training track sharp edges

- Authoritative docs: `training/README.md` (pipeline, RunPod steps) and `training/dataset_schema.json` (consent contract per pair).
- Never commit photos or datasets: `training/data/` is gitignored on purpose; the consented clinic corpus lives outside the repo at `~/firstmate/data/clinic-corpus/` (scraper intake in `<clinic>/raw/`, with `.scraper-cache/` beside it).
- **A pair only counts once it is in the FINISHED tree**, `clinic-corpus/<clinic>/<pair-id>/`. `<clinic>/staging/` and `<clinic>/raw/` are unfinished and invisible to every corpus walk, dataset build and audit - drdanielbarrett, drkolker and sanantonio held 745 pairs in staging and nothing finished until 2026-08-15, and corpus totals quoted anywhere older than that blend the two trees. `training/scripts/emit_corpus.py` is the carry-through (staging -> finished) and the only thing that should write that tree: it re-encodes nothing (`ingest.py` already stripped EXIF, and it refuses rather than strips if an image still carries any), never overwrites an existing pair, and `--report` enumerates every staged pair with its disposition. Always `--dry-run` first. Presence in the finished tree is what makes a pair count, and a documented exclusion does not unmake that: several clinics are ruled out in prose while their pairs sit live in that tree (`training/clinic_watermarks.md`, "Excluded on paper vs excluded in fact").
- Withheld pairs are **retired, not deleted**: `training/retired_pairs.json` enumerates every id kept out of the finished corpus with its ruling and reason (currently the captain's 176 retired laterality labels of 2026-08-15 - 134 sanantonio, 42 drdanielbarrett - plus `sanantonio-24007-oblique-right` withheld as contested, plus the 119 heavenly pairs retired under `retired_watermark` on the captain's 2026-08-15 on-body-watermark ruling, plus 62 pairs over 23 cases and 8 clinics retired under `retired_combined_procedure` on the captain's 2026-08-19 ruling that every combined-procedure case comes out). It is an enumeration and not a rule on purpose: a rule is a live query over staging, and a re-annotation would silently widen a ruling made over a fixed set. `emit_corpus.py` copies each withheld pair into `~/firstmate/data/ba-viz-emit-backlog/quarantine/<reason>/<clinic>/<pair-id>/` and leaves `staging/` untouched; that tree's `MANIFEST.md` is the per-pair record. The registry is the sole authority on a retirement and is keyed by pair id, not by clinic, so `--clinic` cannot be misspelled into letting a retired pair through, and deleting an id from the registry is sufficient to re-emit that pair. The registry gates the EMIT PATH only: an entry keeps a pair from being carried out of `staging/` and does nothing about one already in the finished tree, so removing an already-emitted pair takes a change to the corpus tree itself - do not assume a registry edit is enough. `colville-432-front`/`-side-left` are the worked example: the corrected purity screen caught them a sweep after the original retirement pass, and registering them changed nothing until the pair folders were moved out of the finished tree on 2026-08-19. `--clinic` selects which staged pairs are carried as well as which corpus subdirectory they land in: `ingest.py` stages every clinic into one flat tree, so a staged pair belonging to another clinic is reported as `other-clinic` and left for that clinic's own run rather than misfiled under this one.
- `ClinicConfig.bottom_crop_px` + `crop_bottom()` is how a band burned into the bottom of a side-by-side composite is removed: it trims BOTH halves by the same rows after the split, so the two never differ in framing. Measure the band yourself over every published composite and leave margin - choice's BEFORE/AFTER caption band measures 50-53px and is cropped at 60, leaving 455x441 halves with only ~10% margin over the 400px floor. A band that prints the words BEFORE and AFTER under the respective halves is the label leak in its purest form: the model can satisfy the edit by repainting letters.

The standing captain ruling (2026-08-15) is that a mark clear of breast tissue is acceptable unmasked; one drawn across the breasts rules the clinic out. - **A watermark on ONE half of a pair and not the other is a label leak, and is CROPPED** (captain, 2026-08-19; `.claude/skills/clinic-prospecting/SKILL.md` Screen 4a is the full lesson). An edit model asked to enlarge the breasts can satisfy that by adding or removing the mark, and every quality gate the pipeline has passes it - it surfaces only as a model that learned nothing. Found on four clinics by averaging every frame per half and high-passing the average (bodies cancel, a fixed-position mark survives): tccs on the BEFORE half (743 pairs), roth, camp and wny on the AFTER half (162/299/10). `ClinicConfig.bottom_crop_px` trims BOTH halves equally as the scraper writes the pair (a collection-time crop, so an affected clinic is re-scraped from cache rather than re-emitted) - tccs 130, roth 175, camp 110, wny 60, each measured per clinic and confirmed constant in pixels rather than as a fraction of height. Never transfer one clinic's crop to another, never mask instead (a mask preserves the leak), and exclude what drops below the 400px floor rather than shipping it shrunken (cost: 62 pairs, all of them tccs and camp - roth's and wny's crops are the two largest in the table and cost nothing, so measure the floor rather than assuming a big crop is expensive). Screen for it at INTAKE by comparing before-half against after-half mark incidence; tccs reached the finished corpus twice before anyone compared the halves.

- The Page 1 Solutions purity screen is two-stage and the second stage needs negation handling. The chart's `Procedure Type` field is taken at face value, but a second procedure often reaches only the narrative (`She underwent removal of her old implants and capsulectomy`), so the narrative is screened too - sentence by sentence, skipping any sentence carrying a negation or hypothetical marker. This surgeon's prose routinely explains what the patient DECLINED (`she did not want to have breast lift ... and opted for a breast augmentation alone`) or what was merely discussed as options; without the negation scope that costs four pure ncps cases outright. Bare `removal` is deliberately not a screen term: it reads as a prior unrelated operation about as often as an explant (`a previous tumor removal in the left breast resulted in volume asymmetry`), and the explant cases it would catch are already caught by `capsulectomy`/`explant`/the revision terms. Revisions are rejected under their own class rather than the captain's combined-procedure ruling - a revision's BEFORE photo already carries implants, so its published volume does not describe the corpus's before->after transition - and re-admitting them is one edit to `PAGE1_REVISION_TERMS`.
- **Laterality from a contact sheet is decided by the FAR breast, not by the arm.** CLAUDE.md's convention (chest angled toward the viewer's left => `-left`) is right, but the near-arm phrasing is easy to misapply: on ncps's 480px tiles the arm cue read backwards on two of the first eight cases (10832, 16452) and only a 2x zoom settled them. The cue that does not fail is that **the FAR breast is drawn edge-on against the body's silhouette, on the side the chest faces** - far breast at the frame's right edge => `oblique-right`/`side-right`. Anchored on ncps case 14506, whose tattooed RIGHT forearm is visible in the front view (viewer's left = patient's right) and again as the near arm in its `oblique-right`. The silhouette-centroid statistic confirms a whole clinic's labels as a POPULATION (ncps: left views 0.51-0.52 of frame width, right views 0.47-0.48, front 0.497, matching heavenly's 0.555/0.473) but is useless per case - these frames are individually cropped, so framing dominates pose. The blast-radius re-parse CLAUDE.md requires found 2 changed cases in 2,443 across 32 clinics, both corrections (camp 401's `volume_cc` 285 -> 295; ablavsky 158's 375 moved off the wrong side), which is the worked example of how cheap that proof is to run.
- One clinic's `patient-details` block is not one markup. The shared `influx_swiper` template (lakeshore, marina) publishes the same spec block three ways on the same site - one `<p>` per `Label: value`, one `<li>` per field inside a single wrapper `<p>`, and a bare-value layout that drops the labels and prints each value alone - and the whole block also appears once more as the text of the wrapper `<p>` that parents the real ones. `influx_swiper_parse_case` therefore reads innermost `<p>`/`<li>` only, scans each line for known labels (`INFLUX_FIELD_LABELS`) instead of splitting at the first `": "`, and classifies a label-less line by content. Assume any new gallery does the same before trusting a single-layout parser; all three layouts are pinned by the `lakeshore_case_*` fixtures in `training/tests/test_scrape_gallery.py` (a single-layout parser silently cost 57 cases / 218 pairs here).
- Chart metadata (`placement`, `incision`, `height_cm`, `weight_kg` on `CaseSpecs`) comes from CHART text only, never the clinic's narrative, and only `influx_swiper` and `etna` populate it so far (etna reads it off labelled chart fields like northraleigh's `Placement:`/`Approach:` and roth's `Placement:`/`Incision:`). A narrative that names an incision may be explaining the options rather than reporting this patient's (marina case 7463 walks through dual-plane *and* subglandular), and a published range is not a measurement (sixsurgery's `100 - 149 lbs`, which is why `height_to_cm`/`pounds_to_kg` convert a single documented figure or return `None`).
- Before changing a parser that more than one clinic shares, prove the blast radius: re-parse every cached case for every clinic offline (`PoliteFetcher(cache, offline=True)` + `collect_cases` + `build_meta`, no images) on the base revision and on yours, then diff the rebuilt metas per clinic and per field. `ba-viz-emit-backlog/report.md` section 3.5 is the precedent; `ba-viz-emit-lakeshore/report.md` section 4 is a worked example of what a silent parser miss costs. Three clinics (drkolker, drdanielbarrett, sanantonio) have no cached listing page, so an offline re-parse covers 20 of 23.
- The 2026-08-25 batch is 19 more consented clinics (`clinic-corpus/CONSENT-2026-08-25-PROSPECTED-CLINICS.md`; measured evidence per clinic in `ba-viz-prospect-20-international/report.md`), collected one clinic per agent into its own parser module. `blaine` is the first landed. Two things it established that generalise: a gallery's case number is not necessarily a patient key (Blaine reused three numbers across two generations, so the key is (case number, procedure text) and `build_dataset.py`'s by-patient split depends on getting it right), and **a combined-procedure screen must read the whole figure, not the spec field** - Blaine case #111 publishes a clean `Procedure:` line while all five of its images carry `alt="Mommy Makeover"`.
- `censorship.py` has a false negative worth knowing before trusting it as the only gate: it anchors on a skin silhouette, so a clinic that blurs **tattoos on the arms** rather than faces passes straight through (`blaine-2122-front`, withheld by eye). Several AU/UK clinics publish the same "tattoos, jewellery or other identifiable items blurred" disclaimer, so expect it again. Its known false-POSITIVE family also cost 7 clean Blaine pairs; see `ba-viz-collect-blaine/report.md` section 6 for both, with the boxes drawn and looked at.
- Visual annotation is genuinely expensive at this corpus's scale (3000+ pairs across the 2026-08 batch have no page-documented view): treat it as a distinct, bounded pass per clinic via `annotate_contact_sheets.py` (`--side both` renders each pair's before|after adjacent; `--min-dim 400` drops pairs `ingest.py` would reject anyway), not something to rush or guess through.
- Annotate `front` first and separately: front/oblique/side is a reliable call at 12 pairs per contact sheet, and front is the view most cases publish. Laterality is NOT reliable that way - measured against heavenly's filename-documented labels, blind left/right calls from contact sheets scored ~5/9, with a strong bias toward answering "left". So laterality needs the AGENTS.md landmark rule, and the landmark has to be anchored: the case's own front view fixes the absolute frame (in a front view the patient's left is on the viewer's right), then a mark visible in both the front and the oblique/side tells you which side faces the camera. Without such a mark, leave the pair unannotated - a wrong guess is worse than a missing pair in medical training data. The one thing geometry cannot detect is a MIRRORED source image, which is why the mark is required rather than the pose; a clinic's own text can test that assumption where it names which breast was larger before surgery (choice cases 6 and 13 both matched their front views, i.e. that clinic does not mirror). Evidence like that is worth recording, but it is a captain call to act on, not a licence to relax the rule per pair.
- The corpus convention for laterality (derived from heavenly's `Left-Oblique`/`Right-Oblique` filenames, the only page-documented source): `oblique-left`/`side-left` means the patient's LEFT side faces the camera, which in frame puts the near shoulder/arm on the VIEWER'S RIGHT with the body angled toward the viewer's left. Note that heavenly's per-case labels separate the two groups only as a population (mean body-mass centroid 0.555 vs 0.473 of frame width) and are not independently verifiable case by case, so treat them as the convention's definition rather than as per-case ground truth.
- `ingest.py`'s 400px floor, not annotation, is the binding constraint for several 2026-08 clinics: privateclinic publishes 0 of 77 spec-documented pairs at >= 400px (median 305 after the composite split), marina 20 of 219 (median 288), harrington 50 of 92, mitchellbrown 5 of 15. Check viability with `--min-dim 400` before spending an annotation pass.
- sixsurgery publishes every photo with opaque circles censoring the nipples. The pairs validate fine but training on them would teach the artifact, so they are deliberately not emitted pending a captain ruling; since the censorship gate landed, `ingest.py` also rejects them on its own (97 of 117 cached images).
- `training/scripts/censorship.py` rejects censored/annotated images at ingest (opaque sticker circles and bars, blur bands, mosaic, saturated ink annotations). It is anchored on a skin silhouette, so backdrop watermarks are kept by design. Measured 0.34% false rejects on the corpus (6 of 1782), all drdanielbarrett webp exports so retouched that patches of torso carry no texture at all - if a clean-looking pair is rejected as "texture-free patch of skin", that is the known case, not a new bug. Re-measure with the recipe in the module docstring before touching a threshold. A second false-positive family shows up on corpus-wide re-scans and is worth knowing before running one: a dark backdrop sliver between two body parts (armpit/inner-arm gaps) merged into the skin silhouette, a white divider strip in a stacked-panel composite, or smooth/soft-focus limb skin reading as texture-free. A **light seamless backdrop under soft even light makes that family systematic rather than occasional**: choice trips the gate on 5 of 56 halves (8.9%, ~26x the corpus rate) and 4 of the 5 flagged boxes land on the wall rather than the body - so budget for it on any clinic shooting that way, and look at the flagged box before believing a rejection. `austinweston-01-front` is not one of those - it carries a genuine skin-tone patch over the groin/underwear in both before and after, present in the clinic's own published source and predating this pipeline, left as-is pending a captain call.
That 0.34% is a corpus average and hides a wide per-clinic spread: at sculpted it is 24% (8 of 33 pairs, costing one case all three of its pairs), because the clinic crops at the shoulder line so evenly-lit pore-free deltoid skin lands at the frame edge - every false box there sits at y=6-9.
The same clinic is also the counter-example in the other direction: `censorship.py` MISSED its one genuinely censored case (a mosaic over an identifying mark, mid-torso, in all three views), so neither a pass nor a reject from this gate substitutes for opening the image.
An edge-of-frame box is the cheap discriminator if anyone re-measures the thresholds.
- **`censorship.py` does not catch mosaic pixelation in practice, and `ingest.py` will pass it straight through.** Measured 2026-08-25 on aips: 0 of 182 images flagged, and all 22 visibly-mosaicked pairs were accepted by a real `ingest.py` run, 0 rejected. The clinic burns coarse mosaic over patients' tattoos - arms, sternum, and in several cases across breast tissue. The module's own docstring only ever claimed 72/80 recall on *synthetic* mosaic; real mosaic here is skin-toned and large-celled, so it fails both the `detail-suppressed` hard-edge test and the `burned-in-graphic` saturated-ink test. Do not treat a clean `censorship.py` run as evidence a clinic is uncensored - open the images. What does separate mosaic from smooth skin is a **regular grid of constant cells with hard steps between neighbours** (flatness alone is not enough; lit skin is flat too); a working detector on that principle is at `~/firstmate/data/ba-viz-collect-aips/mosaic.py`. Fixing the module is a corpus-wide change needing the docstring's re-measure recipe, so it is a captain call, not a per-clinic one.
- `training/scripts/deidentify.py` runs NO face detection or blurring, by captain ruling 2026-08-14: the consented clinics contractually guarantee no faces appear in what they publish, which is stronger than anything the code could check. Do not reintroduce a detector, and in particular do not reintroduce a rule that decides by where a blur sits in the frame - position cannot separate a face from a shoulder (`ba-viz-emit-drdanielbarrett/report.md` section 5.2). Every Haar detection ever recorded on this corpus was a false positive (a breast in profile, a shoulder, a hip, hair), and the blur destroyed clean pairs at ten clinics before removal; all but one were recovered 2026-08-15. `deidentify.py`'s module docstring owns the loss detail and `~/firstmate/data/ba-viz-emit-backlog/quarantine/MANIFEST.md` the per-pair disposition - read them there rather than re-deriving counts from the older scout reports. The one pair still outstanding is `heavenly-case23-front`, named here because a pointer cannot reference a key that does not exist: its pair key was never recorded and cannot be derived from the case's two published filenames (`27250527-LACA.jpg` and `27250527-LACA-1.jpg`, neither carrying a view), so it needs a fresh visual view call belonging to the separate view audit.

- As of 2026-08-15 what `deidentify.py` still does is strip EXIF/GPS, by re-encoding the images it writes (`encode_stripped()`); `ingest.py` re-encodes as well. - Corpus images are not uniformly `.jpg`: `~/firstmate/data/clinic-corpus/<clinic>/<pair>/{before,after}.*` mixes `.jpg` (majority), `.jpeg` (mya), `.png`, and `.webp` (drdanielbarrett). Any corpus-wide scan must glob `before.*`/`after.*`, not hardcode `.jpg`, or it silently skips whole clinics.
- **Not every top-level directory in the corpus root is a clinic, and the non-clinic ones are dangerous.** `clinic-corpus/_staging/` is a pre-emit leftover (2026-08-12, predating `emit_corpus.py`) holding 308 pair folders: 245 duplicate live finished pairs, and 63 are heavenly - pairs the captain retired, still sitting there after the retirement emptied `clinic-corpus/heavenly/`. - The finished corpus is the honest source for a dataset build, not `data/clean`: only the corpus tree has the `retired_pairs.json` retirements actually applied. `build_dataset.py` therefore accepts either layout (flat `<pair>/`, or clinic-partitioned `<clinic>/<pair>/`). It splits train/val **by patient, not by pair** - one patient publishes up to five views, so a per-pair split puts the same person in both halves and the val sample grid, the only evidence a run produces, gets scored on a patient the model memorised. It also re-encodes every image on the way out rather than copying bytes: the corpus still contains EXIF-bearing images (14 reached a build before this was added, carrying Photoshop tags and 2024-2025 capture timestamps), and this is the last stage before the images leave the machine for a rented pod.

- A caption is a training LABEL, so an unrecorded field is omitted, never defaulted. The caption states the three axes the product exposes - exact `volume_cc` (never bucketed or rounded; 10 cc granularity at inference depends on the literal figure), `profile`, and `view` - and drops any clause whose field the clinic did not record. Before 2026-08-16 `profile` defaulted to "a balanced profile" on the 61% of pairs carrying none, which taught the model that an unrecorded outcome was a moderate one, and `view` reached no caption at all, which is why the model could not be asked for a projection. `lib/prompt.ts` must omit on the same rule; a caller that knows the photo's view should pass it, since every training caption carries one.
- `lib/prompt.ts` imports `./implants.ts` with the explicit extension and splits type-only bindings into `import type`. - The website's AI provider seam is `AI_PROVIDER` in `.env.example`; `gemini` is the default and the Gemini prompt in `lib/prompt.ts` (`buildEditPrompt`) is intentionally different from the training-caption format.
- Configurator options the corpus doesn't yet back are gated behind an `unbacked` flag on their `lib/implants.ts` entry (currently extra-high profile, teardrop shape) and hidden by default; flip `NEXT_PUBLIC_ENABLE_UNBACKED_IMPLANT_OPTIONS` (`.env.example`) to show them. There is no shared `lib/config.ts`; env vars are read ad hoc at their point of use. Flipping the flag needs no code change, but it does need a rebuild and redeploy to take effect (both for the studio bundle and the `/api/generate` gate): Next.js inlines `NEXT_PUBLIC_*` values into the build output rather than reading them from the running process, unlike `GEMINI_API_KEY`, which is genuinely read at runtime server-side in `app/api/generate/route.ts`.
